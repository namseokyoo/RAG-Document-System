### 2. Relocated Control Buttons to Chat Area
**Problem**: Buttons at bottom of sidebar felt disconnected from chat
**User Feedback**: "사이드바 접기 및 새대화 버튼은 차라리 대화창 왼쪽 위에 놓는게 어떨까??"
**Solution**: Created chat toolbar at top-left of chat area with:
- **◀ 접기** button (80px width) - toggles sidebar
- **➕ 새 대화** button (100px width) - starts new chat
- Button text changes: "◀ 접기" ↔ "▶ 펼치기"

**Benefits**:
- More intuitive - controls are near the content they affect
- Always visible and accessible
- Cleaner sidebar design
- Better use of space
